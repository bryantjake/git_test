How to Export FIT Files from Garmin

  1. Go to connect.garmin.com
  2. Navigate to Activities
  3. Click on the activity you want to export
  4. Click the gear icon (settings) in the top right
  5. Select "Export Original"
  6. The .FIT file will be downloaded to your computer

Note: You can also find FIT files in the GARMIN/ACTIVITY folder on your watch when connected via USB.
